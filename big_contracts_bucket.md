# Big Contracts Bucket: Businesses Moving Major Contracts

**Compiled:** November 2, 2026  
**Focus:** Publicly available data on large U.S. federal government contracts (primary source of transparent "big contract" movement), with notes on statuses and locations where available. Commercial/M&A and AI infrastructure deals are included as supplementary context.  
**Note:** Full "scrape of all aspects" is limited by public data availability, classification, and volume (thousands of awards). This is a curated, structured compilation of recent major activity. Statuses are predominantly "Awarded" for completed notices; many IDIQs have high ceilings with ongoing task orders. Locations refer to place of performance or company/office where specified.

Sources: USAspending.gov, SAM.gov, GovCon Wire, Federal Bid Alerts, Record Flag, and related public reports.

---

## 1. Overview of Large Contract Activity

- In the last 12 months (to early Nov 2026), federal agencies obligated approximately **$757–759 billion** in contracts.
- Top 10 contractors captured ~28% of dollars; top 100 ~59%.
- Department of Defense accounts for ~52% of spending.
- Recent 90-day window: ~26,000+ awards totaling ~$303 billion (many smaller; large ones dominate volume).
- Common statuses: Awarded (definitive contracts, task orders, modifications), IDIQ/multiple-award vehicles (ongoing competition for task orders), OTA (Other Transaction Authority for prototypes/R&D).
- Many "sitting" at: performance sites (bases, labs, HQs), company locations (e.g., Reston VA for many GovCon firms), or in sustainment/production phases.

## 3. Recent Notable Large Awards & Vehicles (Status & Location Highlights)

### Major Awarded / High-Value Contracts
- **Leidos-Parsons-Centerra JV**: Potential 10-year, ~$4B DOE Site Services / Hanford infrastructure contract. **Status:** Awarded. **Location:** Hanford Site (Washington state focus for DOE cleanup).
- **V2X**: Up to $4.3B IDIQ Air Force T-6 Base Supply Support; other awards like $500M Marine Corps logistics, $252M Iraq F-16 support. **Status:** Awarded/ongoing. **Location:** Various bases, including international.
- **Navy WEXMAC TITUS**: Ceiling increase by $45B across 109 companies. **Status:** Multiple-award IDIQ, active. **Location:** Global expeditionary.
- **Salesforce / Computable Insights**: $5.6B 10-year IDIQ Army Agentic AI Enterprise. **Status:** Awarded. **Location:** Enterprise-wide / cloud.
- **Lockheed Martin**: Multiple, including $7.2B Air Force GPS III follow-on, $1B+ Army Future Interceptor OTA, F-35 sustainment ($2.3B Navy), various modifications. **Status:** Awarded/production. **Location:** Various (TX, NJ, etc. for production).
- **Raytheon / RTX**: Large missile/radar awards (e.g., high-volume AMRAAM-related, SPY-6). **Status:** Production/sustainment. **Location:** AZ, CT, etc.
- **Boeing**: KC-46 tanker lots (~$2B+), P-8A, etc. **Status:** Production. **Location:** WA, MO, etc.
- **General Dynamics**: Submarine work (Electric Boat high volume), $922M CENTCOM IT, medium caliber ammo. **Status:** Ongoing. **Location:** CT (Electric Boat), various.
- **Accenture Federal**: $150M+ VA EHR support, $151M Air Force ICBM FENCES (Hill AFB through mid-2030). **Status:** Awarded. **Location:** Hill AFB, UT; various.
- **CACI**: Multiple task orders ($185M Army energy, $250M spectrum, $281M EW, $145M Navy maritime tech). **Status:** Awarded/active. **Location:** Contested/maritime ops.
- **AIS Technical Services**: $137M DOE Portsmouth Infrastructure Support. **Status:** Awarded (Oct 2026). **Location:** OH.
- **Constellis / Centerra**: $1B DOE Savannah River security, $700M Navy base support. **Status:** Awarded. **Location:** Savannah River Site (SC), various bases.
- **Maximus**: $401M contact center operations. **Status:** Awarded.
- **Others**: Tutor Perini $144M DHS, Leidos $342M operational TO, various VA/healthcare, DLA medical, etc.

### High-Ceiling / Ongoing Vehicles
- VA Community Care Network Next Gen: Potential $700B IDIQ (solicitation stage).
- Air Force CFT Labor Augmentation: $7B ceiling, 17 companies.
- Various Navy/Army/Air Force multi-award IDIQs in billions for IT, construction, support.

**Statuses Summary:**
- Most large moves are **Awarded** with options/ceilings for multi-year performance.
- Some in **production/sustainment**, **prototype (OTA)**, or **task order competition**.
- "Sitting" often means active performance at DoD bases, national labs (DOE), VA facilities, or company HQ (many in VA, MD, DC metro, TX, CA).

- **AI Compute Deals**: Anthropic major buyer (e.g., $35B with Lambda in Texas / Hut 8 Beacon Point; Akamai commitments ~$11.6B; Australia leases). Microsoft/Anthropic dominate large Nscale contracts (~$103B total TCV). **Status:** Agreed/committed; financing/location-specific (Texas heavy).

**Recommendation:** For real-time "constant status," monitor SAM.gov, company press releases, and GovCon aggregators. Full scrape requires API access or paid tools due to volume and rate limits.
